System Architecture

FLIGHT
SYSTEMS

Every subsystem engineered for precision — from IMU fusion to real-time motor mixing.

01 / IMU
IMU Sensor Fusion
MPU-6050 gyroscope and accelerometer fused via complementary filter. Real-time roll and pitch at 50 Hz over I2C at address 0x68.
02 / PID
PID Stabiliser
Dual-axis PID with configurable Kp, Ki, Kd. Anti-windup integrator clamping. 20 ms control period at 50 Hz authority.
03 / ESC
Motor Mixing
X-frame quadcopter mixing on D9, D10, D11, D6. PWM 1000–2000 µs via Servo library. Alternated spin directions for yaw authority.
04 / ALT
Altitude Sensing
HC-SR04 ultrasonic on D2/D3. Median-filtered distance in metres. 4 m range with ±3 mm accuracy.
05 / LCD
I2C LCD Display
LiquidCrystal_I2C 16×2 at 0x27. Shows ARM state, altitude, roll, pitch, and throttle. Refreshes at 200 ms.
06 / SAFETY
ARM / DISARM
Push-button on D4 with 50 ms debounce. Hard motor lock when disarmed. PID integrators zero on arm. LED on D13 mirrors arm state.
07 / THR
Throttle Control
10 kΩ potentiometer on A0 maps 0–5V to 1000–2000 µs PWM. Deadband at low throttle. Capped during PID saturation.
08 / SER
Serial Telemetry
Full stream at 9600 baud via USB. Roll, pitch, altitude, throttle, and four motor values every control cycle.
09 / LOOP
Real-Time Loop
Deterministic 50 Hz via micros() timing. Sensor read → PID compute → motor write, all within 20 ms at 16 MHz.
